# Investment Analyst Role

## Role

- Reviews financial logic.
- Checks market score, regime, buy zone, portfolio drift, and risk labels.
- Prevents overconfident trading language.

## Checklist

- Does the system avoid "Buy Now"?
- Are signals framed as planning, potential, and verify-quote references?
- Are yfinance and delayed data warnings visible?
- Are high-volatility stocks treated with stricter rules?
- Are ETFs, stocks, crypto, and gold handled differently?
- Are Taiwan stock quotes marked reference-only if sourced from yfinance?
